How to interpret

Coverage = vaccinated / eligible × 100. The 95% Wilson interval is a binomial uncertainty interval; it does not correct reporting bias or an incorrect denominator.

Dropout = (first dose − final dose) / first dose × 100. Doses must refer to the same cohort and reporting period.
